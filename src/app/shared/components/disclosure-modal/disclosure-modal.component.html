Data Methodology & Insights Disclosure

Predictive Modeling

MarketSense leverages advanced neural networks and Bayesian statistical forecasting to interpret historical market patterns. Our AI models analyze over 4,000 temporal variables simultaneously to identify signal clusters that precede significant market shifts.

Data Integrity

Insights are generated through a hybrid ingestion engine combining Mercer's proprietary institutional capital flow data with high-fidelity external signals, including real-time order book imbalances and macroeconomic indicator releases.

Understanding Prediction Interval

The visual probability cones represent a 95% statistical prediction interval. These shaded areas illustrate the range of potential outcomes based on current volatility; a narrowing cone suggests high-conviction stability, while expanding cones indicate rising regime uncertainty.

Professional Judgment

All model outputs are directional in nature and intended for institutional analysis purposes only. MarketSense provides decision-support intelligence; final strategic allocations must rely on independent professional judgment and internal risk compliance frameworks.
